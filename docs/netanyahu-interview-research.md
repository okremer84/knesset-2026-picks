## Proposed settlement contract

- Proposed betting cutoff and observation start: **18 October 2026, 09:00 Asia/Jerusalem** (`2026-10-18T09:00:00+03:00`). Observation ends **22 October 2026, 22:00 Asia/Jerusalem** (`2026-10-22T22:00:00+03:00`). Freeze actual timestamps before publication; do not silently roll forward a live contract.
- Count the first **new interview** first broadcast in the half-open observation window by an Israeli television or radio outlet. A qualifying interview has a journalist/presenter asking Netanyahu at least two substantive questions and receiving his answers. Live and prerecorded interviews qualify by first public broadcast time, not recording time.
- Include the originating broadcaster's own live stream. Exclude digital-only podcasts, foreign broadcasters, campaign productions, speeches, press conferences, doorstep questions, paid advertisements, reruns and clips from interviews first published before the cutoff. A web-exclusive preview containing two substantive question-and-answer exchanges counts at that first publication time; later broadcast of that same interview is not new.
- Map Kan television interviews to Kan 11 and Kan radio interviews to Israeli radio. Map an original N12/Keshet interview to Channel 12 even if mirrored on mako or YouTube. Redistributors do not become the winning outlet.
- Simultaneous carriage of one outlet's interview belongs to the originating outlet. A genuinely co-produced simultaneous interview with multiple originating outlets belongs to the shared/other television bucket. If separate interviews have indistinguishable first-publication times and the order cannot be established, void; do not choose alphabetically.
- Resolve with the original broadcaster's recording/article plus first-publication or broadcast timestamp, programme name and a short explanation of eligibility. Search snippets alone do not settle. If credible evidence establishes an earlier qualifying interview elsewhere, that earlier interview wins.
- No-interview wins only when the window ends and source coverage is adequate. Unavailable archives, ambiguous ordering or missing coverage require review and potentially voiding; absence from a search engine is not proof of absence.
