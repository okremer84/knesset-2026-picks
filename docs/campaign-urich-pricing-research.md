If the product requires calibrated evidence rather than subjective operator recommendations, leave these unpriced instead of presenting these numbers as measured probabilities.

## Timing and settlement

- Betting closes **2026-10-18 09:00 Asia/Jerusalem** (`2026-10-18T09:00:00+03:00`). No bets accepted at or after that instant.
- Observation is **[2026-10-18 09:00, 2026-10-22 22:00) Asia/Jerusalem**, ending at `2026-10-22T22:00:00+03:00`.
- Eligible publishers: N12/mako news, ynet, Channel 13 News, i24NEWS Hebrew, Kan News, Haaretz/TheMarker, and Globes. Freeze this list in each contract. Commentary, reader comments, social posts and leak-hosting websites are not independent qualifying publications.
- **Yes:** an eligible outlet publishes, during the window, a news report quoting or displaying at least one message explicitly attributed to a direct exchange between Urich and the named person, and the same message content was not reported by an eligible outlet before the window. The report must concern public/political work, rather than unrelated intimate or personal information. A group-chat excerpt qualifies only when it identifies both people as directly exchanging the quoted messages; membership alone is insufficient.
- **Not enough:** a person's name appearing in someone else's exchange; an empty placeholder; promises of forthcoming material; an opinion about the person; a denial alone; a new headline, translation, screenshot crop, or repost of the same known message.
- **No:** the window ends with no qualifying report, and monitored-source coverage is demonstrably complete enough to establish that result. Search-engine silence alone is not proof of No.
- **Void:** missing coverage cannot be repaired, publication time cannot be established at the boundary, or novelty/direct-exchange attribution remains genuinely unresolved after review. Do not interpret inaccessible pages as No. An outlet retracting the qualifying attribution before operator confirmation invalidates that evidence; review remaining evidence before choosing No or void.
- Settlement judges whether the defined report was published, not underlying authenticity. Every approval card must include the outlet URL, publication timestamp, minimal relevant passage, the named participants, and a comparison against the pre-window exclusion baseline. Keep private identifiers and irrelevant personal details out of the card.
